# TD3 motion study

Current working geometry uses the owner's dimensioned SolidWorks screenshots,
superseding the older 135/135 cm reach assumptions and earlier reset-pose notes
below. See [drawing geometry](DRAWING_GEOMETRY.md) for dimensions and unresolved
mount geometry. The preserved stable v0.1 snapshot is unchanged.

Gold surfaces mark pivots and slide guides. **Show pivots & slides** is a checkbox
that controls the 11 actuator markers and gold slide highlights. With it off,
an actuator whose coordinate changes appears for two seconds after its last
change; steel guides stay visible. Cylinder-end and pendulum markers
are omitted. Slide points represent carriage/reference positions, not rotary pins.
With **Show dimensions** enabled, click two visible gold points: A is cyan and B
orange. Leaders show horizontal
ground-plane distance, signed plumb rise B minus A, center distance and inclination
from horizontal. The pair updates live during movement. A third pivot starts a
new pair; **Clear pivot measurement** clears it. Turning points off/on also clears
the pair. Reset pose now uses boom 25°, joint 28°, Z-slide
151.75 cm, with pitch/roll zero and mast plumb. Track reset also uses pitch zero.
The owner-confirmed reference COR height is exactly 2324.7 mm (232.47 cm) at this
level-base/trunnion pose. WP coincides with COR at mast 195 cm / lower crowd
160 cm and stays on its axis as those slides move; see DRAWING_GEOMETRY.md.

## Run on this laptop

Open **index.html** directly in Chrome, or double-click **Launch Model.cmd**.
The viewer is bundled into a classic script, so direct `file:///` viewing works
without a server or internet connection. The launcher rebuilds using Node.js if
available and then opens the local HTML file. Dependencies are installed here.

Optionally run `node server.mjs` and open http://127.0.0.1:8313 instead. Ctrl+C
stops that server. The current launcher does not start a background server.

For viewing on another laptop, copy the contents of `dist/` and open its
`index.html`; no Node.js installation is required. To develop/rebuild, install
Node.js LTS and run `npm install` or `pnpm install --frozen-lockfile`, then
`node build.mjs`. pnpm-lock.yaml records the dependency resolution. npm does not
read the pnpm lockfile. Generated viewer.js, reference/ and dist/ are ignored by
Git and regenerated by the build; they are already present on this laptop.

## Use

Dimension/ET controls are now a compact top-right card. With dimensions enabled,
click an actuator for CAD-style arrows and labels beside it; they track the camera.
Z-slide movement updates ET size, stance, reveal and screw/leg outlines live;
out-of-table pin heights are flagged. The ground direction label is SOUTH / REAR.

New: **Show dimensions** enables plumb COR/pin heights. Click an actuator for
its length/span and world-horizontal angle; lengths are in cm. **ET Plumb mode**
shows plumb screw tops, each-side offset and full stance in inches, selecting a
listed drawing size with valid reveal closest to 9 inches. See [measurement conventions and limits](MEASUREMENTS.md).

Current profile: **3.2 = machine family 3, version 2**. Position controls use
axesInit initialization ranges, with the source's live coupled crowd top limit;
upper zero is allowed as a touching-edge preview. Lower-crowd minimum uses the
version-2 machineInit branch (37 cm). See [source range mapping](LIMITS.md).
Both rotary controls now set signed demonstration RPM for continuous rotation
in either direction. Stop rotaries halts them; Reset also restores angular pose.
Angle position stops are not applied to the rotaries. This supersedes earlier
descriptions of rotary pose sliders and demonstration-only position ranges.

To share, send [the portable ZIP](releases/TD3-model-3.2.zip). Recipients extract
the entire archive and open index.html. Nothing to install. SHARING.txt in the
package explains the controls and limitations. Source is not included in the ZIP;
it contains the bundled viewer and two CAD references, with the Three.js license.

Drag to orbit, scroll to zoom, right-drag to pan. Camera buttons select side, end,
top, or isometric. Fit reframes the current pose. Sliders or numeric inputs set
each joint directly; focusing a control highlights its downstream assembly.
Reset pose restores all joints, while keeping your camera view. Pivot axes can
be toggled. The side and angled CAD references are linked in the notes panel.

Position ranges come from source initialization; most geometry and rotary demo speeds remain estimates. This is direct pose control,
not a simulation of valve response, speeds, interlocks, collisions or soil.

## Geometry / hierarchy

Current dimensions, datums and limitations are maintained in
[DRAWING_GEOMETRY.md](DRAWING_GEOMETRY.md). The dimensioned boom link is 118.38 cm;
the swing/yaw spacing is 142.929445384 cm at the drawing pose; pitch-to-drill
spacing is 93.32 cm. The old 135/135 cm arm approximation is superseded.
Machine axes are forward/right/up, mapped to render (X, Z, Y); heights use the
shared ground plane. See the drawing record for the lift-angle offsets.

Pitch carries the Z-slide backbone and its moving white member. The white member
now connects to its drawing-positioned rotator support. Antennas follow Z-slide;
rotator roll carries the mast and crowds. Mast slide carries the whole mast;
lower crowd carries upper crowd, whose position adds a relative bearing-edge gap.
The two rotary/tool axes remain coaxial through these transforms.

Crowd sleds touch at upper=0. Source sled dimensions and centralizer/chuck datums
replace arbitrary offsets; a third drifter sled follows upper crowd. Live coupled
limits retain 5 cm to the modeled rail top. The source 37 cm lower minimum keeps
the bottom bearing 9.06 cm above the guide bottom. The effective rail envelope
follows the active source clearance arithmetic; physical endpoints and the
stacked-near-top expectation still need confirmation against the rig.

Bodywork, cylinder attachment points, component cross-sections, antenna spacing
and holder shape remain schematic. Ground crossings at very low combined poses
are documented; this direct-pose viewer does not execute hydraulic dynamics,
soil contact or the PLC state machine. Configuration is in config.js, transforms
in kinematics.js, crowd limits in crowd-geometry.js and meshes in scene.js.

## Checks and packaging

`node --test tests/*.test.mjs` checks physical sign examples, reference reach,
coupled slides/crowds, tool axes, endpoints, and reset. `node build.mjs` assembles
an offline static viewer in ignored `dist/`, plus viewer.js and reference/ for
the source-folder entry. esbuild bundles the JavaScript modules into a classic
script so Chrome can open the HTML directly. CAD reference images are copied
with the portable viewer. Build before tests: startup tests check the bundle,
its classic-script syntax and portable-file completeness. esbuild is only a
build dependency, not needed for viewing.

Validation evidence and remaining limitations are recorded in
[the implementation check](../docs/verification/2026-11-02-standalone-model.md).

ET Plumb entry aligns the mounted cap to COR at mast 195 cm / lower crowd 160 cm.
Notes beside each slide show its reference and current required alignment value.
Slides are freely adjustable after entry; Align holder restores the reference.
The cap moves with the lower-crowd holder, replacing the interim fixed-COR target.
The 160 cm lower-crowd reference is the owner-selected source declaration default,
not a readback of persistent rig calibration.

## November 3 visual and reset update

Upper rotary demonstration speed is now -200 to +200 rpm (lower stays +/-60).
The new **Reset all · Track defaults, mast plumb** button stops both rotaries,
resets their visual angles, and sets the nominal non-GPS Track pose: boom swing
0°, boom lift 25°, joint lift 28°, yaw 0°, X-slide 15 cm, Z-slide 104.86 cm,
mast slide 185 cm, lower crowd 160 cm, upper crowd 245 cm, roll 0°.
Pitch is 0° to make the mast world-plumb with the current drawing-to-control offsets.
This is a viewer preset, not execution of the PLC state machine.
The existing Reset pose button retains the earlier assembly-reference pose.

Source: 530v2 Application/Global Variables/StatePositions.gvl and
POUs/MACHINE_LEVEL/machineInit.pou; actuator BOOM_SWING, YAW, X_SLIDE,
Z_SLIDE, MAST_SLIDE and LOWER_CROWD Track paths. X-slide can instead use 10 cm
with GPS targeting active. Upper crowd Track actuation is explicitly disabled
by AND FALSE in UPPER_CROWD.pou/UPPER_CROWD.pou: the preset uses its declared
245 cm nominal value for a deterministic reset, not a claim that Track moves it.
Source pitch travel lean (8°) is deliberately replaced by model-plumb pitch.
Actual persistent settings and deployed machine configuration remain unknown.

Mast top is closed with a red plate. Following the November 3 photo correction,
two GPS pucks sit on black, braced arms projecting to either side near the top
of the moving Z-slide. They follow Z-slide/pitch motion, independently of mast
slide and roll. Arm shapes and antenna spacing remain visual estimates.
The machine screw and ET screw-top tubes/rims have 4-inch (10.16 cm) outside
diameter, including the simplified flight/rim envelopes.
**Desert environment** toggles a very light brown ground color and six translucent
green scrub bushes. The ground height, grid, ET calculations and scale figure
are retained; translucent leaves do not write depth, so the grid shows through.
