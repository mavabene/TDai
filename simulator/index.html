Geometry & reference notes
Dimensioned reference: boom 25°, level trunnion (joint 28°), level base, pitch/roll zero and Z-slide 151.75 cm give COR height 232.47 cm. At mast slide 195 cm and lower crowd 160 cm the holder WP coincides with COR. Other mast/crowd positions move WP along the mast axis through COR.
Toggle pivot points to show/hide the actuator axes and slide reference markers. With points off, a moved actuator's point appears for two seconds. Enable dimensions and select two visible points for ground-plane distance, plumb rise and angle from A to B. A third selection starts a new pair.
